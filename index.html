How to find the way

  1. 01

    Turn a mirror. Click it, or focus it and press Enter. Each turn bends the light by a quarter circle.

  2. 02

    Follow the starlight. The beam travels in a straight line until a mirror turns it. Collect every star along the route.

  3. 03

    Reach the moonflower. There is no timer. Reset any time, or ask for a little nudge when you need one.
